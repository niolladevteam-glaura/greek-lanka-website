In today’s business world, companies cannot be built to depend on individuals. They must be built on strong systems run by skilled people.

Over the past months, we have been quietly investing in strengthening this foundation. We started with process thinking helping our team understand why consistency, structure, and standardization matter.

## Our First People Development Session: “Who Am I?”

This week, we continued that journey with our first People Development Session: **“Who Am I?”**

This session wasn’t about job titles or daily tasks. It was about asking:

- Who am I in this organization?
- What value do I bring?
- What responsibility do I carry?
- Who do I want to become?

Because systems create structure, but people create ownership.

We are not building a company that depends on a few individuals. We are building a company that can grow, scale, and sustain itself.

This is only the beginning, and we are proud to take this journey together as one team.
